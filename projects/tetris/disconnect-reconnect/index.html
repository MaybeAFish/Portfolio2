Disconnect & Reconnect

I handled the multiplayer edge cases that made the game resilient when players dropped out or rejoined mid-session.

  • Protected singleplayer and lobby states from abrupt disconnects, including auto-pause and timeout logic.
  • Created graceful reconnect flows so late joiners could re-enter without breaking the active match.
  • Managed resume timing, waiting conditions, and the disconnected board state so a player could recover before dying.
  • Coordinated competitive-mode pause logic and synchronized player feedback to keep the match state readable and fair.

I designed and implemented the disconnect and reconnect flows for the game's multiplayer sessions. I tested a wide range of edge cases and worked closely with the Creative Director to discuss and propose solutions for situations I identified during development.

  • Singleplayer disconnects:
    • Pauses the game if you're playing alone.
    • Shows a 20-second timeout to reconnect if there is atleast one other person waiting in the lobby.
  • Mid-game reconnects:
    • Pauses the game if you're playing alone.
    • Shows a 20-second timeout inside your playingfieldto reconnect if there is atleast one other person waiting in the lobby.
  • Mid-game reconnects: allowing disconnected players to rejoin an active match without disrupting the other players.
  • Mid-game joining: providing appropriate feedback to new players who join while a match is already in progress, instructing them to wait until the current game ends.
  • Competitive disconnects: pausing the entire match with a 10-second countdown and clearly communicating which player disconnected.
  • Reconnect timing: when a disconnected player reconnects during the countdown, a 3-second timer gives them time to return before the match resumes.
  • Disconnected players: if the timeout expires, the player's board visually indicates that they are disconnected, while still allowing them to continue playing if they reconnect before their board reaches its game-over state.
  • Multiple disconnects: handling additional disconnects while another player's reconnect timer is active, ensuring the game waits for all affected players before resuming.
