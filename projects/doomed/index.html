Doomed

Doomed is a first-person boomer shooter developed in Unreal Engine 5. The project had already been through three development teams before my team took over during our Gameplay Engineering semester.


My main contributions for this project:

  • Stylization
    • Cel- & outline shading
    • Lighting
    • Fog
    • HUD
  • Combat
    • Melee
    • Weapon-wheel accessibility
    • Enemy projectiles reflecting off terrain
    • Ability learning
    • Expected reload behaviour
  • Horde Mode
  • Project Structure

What I've learned during this project:

  • The role as Project Lead
  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Blueprints
  • Git LFS
  • Canva presentations
  • Communicating design decisions through presentation and documentation
  • What the hell a boomer shooter even is
