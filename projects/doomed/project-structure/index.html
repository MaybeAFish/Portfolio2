Project Structure

When we started the project, we immediately noticed that the existing project structure would be very difficult to maintain and scale.

  • Complex and inconsistent folder structure.
  • 28 GB project size.
  • Unused blueprint references.
  • Large amounts of unused assets, including:
    • Dozens of VFX.
    • Hundreds of models.
    • Thousands of materials.

The project had been developed by three previous student teams, who hadn't invested a lot of attention to maintaining the scalability of the project.

As team lead, I decided to spend the first two weeks of development reorganizing and cleaning the project, including my free time. I restructured the entire project structure, removed unnecessary assets and references and set collaboration rules for each other.

Alongside the technical work, I coordinated tasks and deadlines within the team and communicated our most important design decisions to the product owner.
