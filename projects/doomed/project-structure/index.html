Project Structure

The project structure f****** SUCKED.

  • Incredibly complex folder structure.
  • 28 GB project.
  • Random unused asset flips.
    • Dozens of VFX.
    • Hundreds of models.
    • Thousands of materials.

  • Random trash everywhere.

This was three previous studentteams thatt didn't really pay a lot of attention on mainting the scalability of the project.

And I personally know how frustrating it is to work in such an environment, as it slows down your performance by A LOT. So, for myself, for my team, and for future teams, I decided to work two weeks at the start of the project, including my free time, to clean it up. I believe that this cleaup has greatly increased our productivity.
