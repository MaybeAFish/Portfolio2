My Design Reasoning Behind this Portfolio

The main idea behind my portfolio

I wanted this portfolio to not waste any time. I'm a fan of "Show, don't tell".

  • My portfolio doesn't have a homepage. You immediately start off at the projects page.
  • This projects page has a small banner with only the most absolutely necessary information like socials and game designer & developer, with only when on hover hsowing extra info wh
  • I tried having as little generic portfolio language as possible.
  • I made my projects self explanatory with only media and as little as possible text in the media.
  • The best part of each of my case studies should be summarized in just the title.

However, this isn't a rule you can forever rely on, as I'm mostly selling my game design skills on my portfolio, which is something that needs to be well explained.

The goal was not to have less content. It was to make sure the content that remains actually has a purpose.

Articles titles

My article titles used to be very boring. This was before I heard people mention to sell your "impact" on your portfolio. I tried achieving this by creating more interesting title articles by thinking what I actually achieved with my work.

  • From "Creating Multiplayer Stability in a Tetris Game" to "Reducing Player Frustration in Tetris Airconsole"
  • From "Designing Gamified Physiotherapy Exercises in VR" to "Making Physiotherapy Exercises Fun"
  • From "Iterating on Doomed's HUD" to "Making Doomed's Visuals Work Together"
  • From "Improving Doomed's Onboarding" to "Making Doomed Easier to Learn"
  • From "Polishing Doomed's Combat with Playtesting" to "Making Doomed's Combat Feel Better"
  • From "Designing a Play of the Game for a Karaoke Game" to "Finding the Most Fun 15 Seconds of a Karaoke Performance"
  • From "Environmental Hazards" to "Forcing the Player to Reposition in Interesting Ways"

I realized that I could shape the entire thought process people have when reading my articles, by simply changing the title even though the content remaining the same.
