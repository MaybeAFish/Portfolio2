Designing a Karaoke "Play of the Game" System


Game Systems I've designed & implemented

Karaoke Play of the Game

I designed a system that identifies the most memorable 15 seconds of a player or group's performance. Different moments throughout the song were given different weights based on how entertaining or significant they were, with the highest-scoring segment being replayed at the end of the session.

The system had to respect special edge cases such as when the game is paused, or when someone has a terrible internet connection.

Karaoke Game

I designed an extensive Play of the Game system while keeping the player experience in mind. I did this by performing the following steps:

  1. Identifying the different motivations people might enjoy while playing karaoke.
  2. Determining which in-game recorded events could realistically represent those motivations.
  3. Determining the weights of those events by surveying the target audience.
  4. Determining how the weights should be used when calculating what the moment should be.
  5. Implementing it, and then tons of playtesting to make some number adjustments.
