Somehow Fitting Combat in a Match 3 Game


Gameplay loop

  • The player is prompted with a Quick Time Event (QTE) to earn moves
  • The player uses those moves in a match-3 field to accumulate damage
  • The player decides which enemy to attack to use the damage on
  • The enemy(s) strike back
  • Repeat

Combining items to become a stronger samurai

After winning battles, the player could combine items by match-3'ing them to make the item stronger. Items could increase movementspeed during fights or allow the player to reach further on the board.
