Game Loops

I believe that every game has at least one game loop: a repeating sequence of actions that keeps the player engaged which drives the entire game's experience.

  • In Tetris,
    • The player receives a tetromino
    • The tetromino is placed
    • The game evaluates the resulting board
    • Repeat
  • In VALORANT,
    • Preparation time
    • The barrier drops
    • A combat system happens
    • The round ends when a wincondition is reached
    • Repeat
  • In Life is Strange,
    • The player is presented with a new part of the narrative
    • The player may explore the environment and interact with characters or objects
    • The player makes decisions that influence the experience
    • The story progresses
    • Repeat

Here are some game loops I've designed:


Turn-based match-3 game

  • The player is prompted with a Quick Time Event (QTE) to earn moves
  • The player uses those moves in a match-3 field to accumulate damage
  • The player decides which enemy to attack to use the damage on
  • The enemy(s) strike back
  • Repeat

Doomed's Horde Mode

  • The round starts
  • After a short duration, enemies start spawning into the arena
  • The player must kill all the enemies
  • The player occasionally gains a new weapon
  • Repeat
