League of Legends Rune Concept

I would like for Time Warp Tonic to receive a rework, as I believe its value is too niche and difficult for players to understand, limiting the Inspiration Tree's potential from offering more interesting strategic choice(s).

Biscuit Delivery sit in the exact same row, which already provide players with more immediately understandable benefits of early-game sustain.

That's why I'd want to rework Time Warp Tonic into:

"Pick Your Poison"

When picking this rune, the player would receive two potions at minute 3. The player then has to choose which one they want to drink.

Each potion provides a combat benefit and a combat drawback, meaning the player has to adapt their choice based on their current situation to get the most value out of this rune.

After choosing a potion, the player receives another pair of potions two minutes later. After choosing from that pair, they receive one final pair two minutes after that.

Potion concepts:

  • First set of potions
    • Apple Juice: Increase damage (10%) but reduce healing received (-80%) for 30s.

      Apple Juice thought process:

      Considering you receive the first potion pair at minute 3, it'd mean mean that the reduced healing should be balanced around healing/sec numbers, as that is the primary source of healing for all champions in the earlygame. However, I don't consider making the -80% antihealing specific for healing/sec as that would cause problems; Briar wouldn't have any drawbacks considering she already has no heal/sec in her kit.

      Another problem could be the fountain. If you were to recall while under this potion's effect, you might have a hard time regenning health before you could walk back to base. That's why I would also suggest giving the fountain "pure healing", similar to it's already "pure damage" mechanic. "Pure healing" would ignore grievous wounds effects, healing the player like normal. I am aware that giving the fountain "pure healing" could influence champion balancing with global ults that have bought greavous wounds (I.E. Karthus), which at the same time could reduce player frustration. However, if giving the fountain pure healing isn't an option, I would consider changing the -80% antiheal effect to be specific to selfhealing.

      It's also worth nothing that junglers could use this potion to make their clear potentially 2-4 seconds faster without any drawback, (as most of the time, they don't really care about the reduced healing), but most junglers would then have to give up either Cosmic Insight (-13.73s on smite), or cash back (200g–1000g).

My design thinking:

  • I wanted it to be another potion rune, as it would fit naturally between the other consumable runes: Biscuit Delivery and Triple Tonic.
  • Rather than relying on an advantage that's difficult to perceive, I wanted to create an understandable player choice that adds strategic depth, similar to other runes in the Inspriation tree like Hextech Flashtraption.
  • I decided on three sets of potions. Not only because receiving choices (can) make something more fun and engaging, but also because it would match the other runes in the same row, which both provide three consumables.
