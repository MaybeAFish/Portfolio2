Giving a 2D Space Invaders Game a Reason to Exist

Environmental hazard

The movement in Space invaders is traditionally single dimensional

I made it 2d...

... the player ended up having no reason to move in the y axis

I designed various semi-random hazard mechanics such as asteroids, supernovas or solar rays.

I made the asteroids always first in slot, as they fly the slowest and are easiest to understand.

Making Enemies Use Positioning to Survive


Hiding eyeballs

I designed a mechanic which made the eyeball enemies hide from the player by positioning themselves between meatshields and the player.

If there aren't any meatshields left, the eyeballs would seemingly gather the courage to rush towards the player. This was the very first game I had ever made; Had I designed this today, I would've made them scatter instead and then fly away from the player if they get too close to keep the idea that they're nonconfrontational enemies.
