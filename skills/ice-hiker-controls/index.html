Designing The Ice Hiker's Camera & Animation System

Controls System

I designed and implemented the main controls system for The Ice Hiker. The system rotates the camera based on mouse movement, uses raycasts to prevent the camera from clipping through terrain and moves the player in the direction the camera is facing. The character would only look in the camera direction when the player performed an action.

I designed it this way because I wanted players to be able to see the character's face to create more of an emotional attachment without making the camera difficult to control. I verified this through A/B testing, where every player rated my system higher than Unity's default cinemachine version.

Looking back, the camera occasionally clips through terrain in a distracting way. I believe that the raycast should have been a "spherecast", which would've allowed me to make the camera more sensitive to prevent potential clippings.


Animation System

I also designed and implemented a custom animation system for The Ice Hiker because Unity's Animator Controller felt limited and difficult to scale. My system lets actions easily trigger animations based on their "priority". It also supported custom transition times between animations. Some animations feel better if they play immediately, such as throwing the pickaxe, while others look better when they happen more gradually, like falling.
