Designing The Ice Hiker's Controls System

Controls system

I designed the controls system for the ice hiker. A system which rotates the camera when the mouse is moved, lets the camera collide with terrain using raycasts, and lets the player move in the direction the camera is looking at.

I designed it this way, because I wanted the player to look at the face of the character without the camera being difficult to control.

Had it been first person, the player would have a hard time orientating themselves while flying and swinging.

I had done an A/B test with 2 camera systems;

Looking back, the camera occasionally clips in an annoying way when the camera is close to terrain. The raycast when camera's colliding with terrain shouldve been much more sensitive so that this doesnt happen.


Animation system

I also designed an animation system for the Ice Hiker, which allows for any mechanic to play an animation as long as that animations has a higher priority than the currently playing one.
