Designing The Ice Hiker's Camera & Movement System

Controls system

I designed and implemented the controls system for the Ice Hiker. The system rotates the camera based on mouse movement, uses raycasts to prevent the camera from clipping through terrain and moves the player in the direction the camera is facing. The character would only look in the camera direction when the player performed an action.

I designed it this way because I wanted players to be able to see the character's face to create more of an emotional attachment without making the camera difficult to control. I verified this through A/B testing, where every player rated my system higher than the previous version.

Looking back, the camera occasionally clips through terrain in a distracting way. I believe that the raycast should have been a "spherecast", which would've allowed me to make the camera more sensitive to prevent potential clippings.


Animation system

I also designed and implemented a custom animation system for the Ice Hiker because Unity's Animator Controller felt limited and difficult to scale. My system allowed movement mechanics to trigger animations based on their priority, with a new animation playing whenever its priority was higher than or equal to the currently playing one.
