Designing Multiplayer Stability for Tetris


Tetris Airconsole

During interal playtests, players encountered confusing situations when someone disconnected, reconnected, or joined at an unexpected moment. These situations would interrupt the game or leave players unsure about what was happening.

I treated these as player experience problems rather than technical problems. I designed and proposed the desired behaviour to the Creative Director before we'd start discussing the details, including reconnect timers, pausing behaviour, multiple disconnected players and joining during different game states.
