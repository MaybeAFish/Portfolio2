INFECTED

A 2-player puzzle game where one of you is infected. Solve puzzles while avoiding touching eachother.

The game was made inside a Habbo retro server. I designed some of the more complex levels and I developped all of the logic using WIRED.

Last time I checked it was played over 38.000 times, but only a few managed to beat all 35 levels and win a pixelart achievement I've made.
